Basic requirements

Have these ready before applying.

  • Be legally eligible to work as a delivery partner in your area.
  • Have a working mobile phone and reachable number.
  • Know local streets and nearby villages.
  • Have a bicycle, scooter, or other suitable transport.

  • Be polite, punctual, and handle customer orders carefully.
